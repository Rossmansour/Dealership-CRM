Recon Domus

Get cars from "just arrived" to the front line faster. Every car moves through your own steps with a time goal, so you see what's stuck and where.

  • New and used split, all the way through your reports and ADR
  • Every car in stock goes into recon on its own; trades stock in when the deal is delivered
  • Work items with parts, labor, and approvals; mechanical work goes to service as an internal RO
  • A pull-out appraisal panel shows the gross while the manager approves work

Inventory Domus

Price every car to the market and appraise with confidence.

  • Market pricing: similar cars for sale near you, adjusted for miles, with a suggested price
  • Your rules: target % of market, aging steps, a gross floor -- and optional auto-pricing once a day
  • Appraisals with market comparables, recon planning, and an offer calculator
  • Inventory with photos, key tracking, and days in stock
Available now

Why one system matters

One customer, everywhere

The customer your salesperson adds is the same one on the deal, the credit app, the service RO, and the reports. No re-typing, no mismatches.

One car, start to finish

A trade gets appraised, stocked in, reconditioned, priced to the market, and sold -- and its recon cost lands on the car automatically.

One set of numbers

The gross on the deal recap is the same gross on the dashboard and the same gross commissions are paid on.

The right access for each role

Salespeople, managers, F&I, service, and parts each see what they need. Every change is kept in an audit log, and sensitive data is encrypted.

Questions

Do I have to replace my DMS?

No. Many stores start with Recon Domus or Inventory Domus alongside their current DMS. Accounting is coming next, and when it's ready DealerDomus can replace the DMS entirely.

Does it work for independent used car stores?

Yes -- recon, market pricing, appraisals, CRM, and desking all work the same for independents and franchise stores.

Is our customers' information safe?

Social Security and license numbers and scanned documents are encrypted in the database, each person only sees what their role allows, and every change is logged with who made it.

What does it cost?

Founding stores use it free for 60 days, then keep a locked-in early price. Book a demo and we'll put together pricing for the modules you want.

Does it work on a phone or tablet?

Yes. It runs in the browser on computers, tablets, and phones -- nothing to install.
